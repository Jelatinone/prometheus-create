## 1. Outcome and acceptance

**Objective:** simplify the existing editor and project-wide UI while preserving working camera/video, generation, persistence and layer behavior. **Visible flow:** use the left Object/Camera/Generate/Source icon rail, inspect details on the right, perform mode-specific actions from the bottom toolbar, and open small popovers when needed. No permanent timeline or tutorial paragraphs. Generate makes choosing/changing a captured frame and starting a new selection obvious.

**Edges:** a retained generation batch cannot silently resubmit or lose durable job references; selecting a different decoded video frame must actually change the preview and stored source time; source time stays separate from take time. Critical labels such as Estimated, real job states, save conflicts and errors remain concise and truthful.

**Out of scope:** app/api or auth/backend rewiring, new providers/contracts, per-object timeline/clip-range editing, agentic playback, coaching, new paid calls and publication. **Cut:** optional UI animation/hover/decoration; preserve all core actions and the 08:00 feature freeze.

### Subpass A: frame selection (done)

**Bounded plan:** check both read-only findings in the running app; fix only what blocks choosing a frame; no provider submit.

**Findings:**
- **Generate frame lock: confirmed.** Any retained batch disabled the frame picker and Refresh until "Clear and start over" at the bottom, so a finished batch made the frame look unchangeable.
- **Video seek: no hang reproduced** on a small H.264 clip, but `seekVideo` could wait forever (no same-position path, no error or timeout), which would leave the Capture panel busy.

**Delivered:**
- Generate: the frame picker and Refresh stay enabled unless a job is running. Choosing another frame keeps the current frame's searches and results (stored per frame, by frame ID) and brings them back when that frame is chosen again; a frame with kept results is marked "has results". Finished meshes re-read their job; nothing is resubmitted.
- `seekVideo` always settles: at once when the frame is already shown, with an error if the clip is not loaded or fails to decode, and after a 5 s timeout otherwise.
