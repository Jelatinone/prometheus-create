### Subpass A: frame selection (done)

**Bounded plan:** check both read-only findings in the running app; fix only what blocks choosing a frame; no provider submit.

**Findings:**
- **Generate frame lock: confirmed.** Any retained batch disabled the frame picker and Refresh until "Clear and start over" at the bottom, so a finished batch made the frame look unchangeable.
- **Video seek: no hang reproduced** on a small H.264 clip, but `seekVideo` could wait forever (no same-position path, no error or timeout), which would leave the Capture panel busy.

**Delivered:**
- Generate: the frame picker and Refresh stay enabled unless a job is running. Choosing another frame keeps the current frame's searches and results (stored per frame, by frame ID) and brings them back when that frame is chosen again; a frame with kept results is marked "has results". Finished meshes re-read their job; nothing is resubmitted.
- `seekVideo` always settles: at once when the frame is already shown, with an error if the clip is not loaded or fails to decode, and after a 5 s timeout otherwise.
